About North Star Bakery

A neighborhood bakery built around fresh food and community.

Our Story

North Star Bakery was created to be a welcoming neighborhood bakery where customers can find fresh breads, pastries, and cakes made with care.

We believe that good food can bring people together. From a loaf of bread for a family meal to pastries for a morning commute, our goal is to provide fresh baked goods that our local community can enjoy.

Our bakery serves local customers, commuters, families, and event planners looking for fresh baked goods for everyday occasions and special events.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe that quality ingredients are an important part of making great baked goods. North Star Bakery focuses on selecting ingredients that support the flavor and quality of our breads, pastries, and cakes.

We keep our approach simple: use quality ingredients, prepare our products with care, and bake with the customer in mind.

Our focus on handmade baking helps us maintain the warm and personal feeling of a neighborhood bakery.

Staff Highlights

Our Bakers

Our bakers are dedicated to preparing breads, pastries, and cakes with attention to detail. They work behind the scenes to make sure customers have fresh products to choose from.

Our bakery team welcomes customers, helps answer questions, and assists with product and pre-order requests.

Our Community

North Star Bakery values the customers and neighbors who make our bakery part of the local community. We are proud to serve families, commuters, and customers planning special events.
