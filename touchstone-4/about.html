About North Star Bakery

North Star Bakery was established to be a welcoming neighborhood bakery where customers can find freshly baked breads, pastries, and cakes crafted with utmost care.

Our Story

North Star Bakery was created to be a welcoming neighborhood bakery where customers can find fresh breads, pastries, and cakes made with care.

We firmly believe that good food has the power to unite people. Whether it is a warm loaf of bread shared during a family meal or a delightful pastry enjoyed during a morning commute, our mission is to offer our local community fresh, delicious baked goods that they can savor.

Our bakery caters to local customers, commuters, families, and event planners seeking fresh baked goods for both everyday occasions and special events.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

North Star Bakery believes that using high-quality ingredients is crucial in creating exceptional baked goods. We meticulously select ingredients that complement the flavors and enhance the quality of our breads, pastries, and cakes.

We keep our approach simple: use quality ingredients, prepare our products with care, and bake with the customer in mind.

Our focus on handmade baking helps us maintain the warm and personal feeling of a neighborhood bakery.

Staff Highlights

Our Bakers

Our bakers are dedicated to preparing breads, pastries, and cakes with attention to detail. They work behind the scenes to make sure customers have fresh products to choose from.

Our bakery team welcomes customers, helps answer questions, and assists with product and pre-order requests.

Our Community

North Star Bakery cherishes the customers and neighbors who make our bakery an integral part of the local community. We take pride in serving families, commuters, and individuals planning special events.
